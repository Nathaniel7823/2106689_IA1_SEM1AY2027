The Book

This book is about some short stories that teach people about saving, investing and protecting their money. Read the PDF

info I read the chapters "7 Cures for a Lean Purse" and "5 Laws of Gold" and wrote what I took from each one.

7 Cures for a Lean Purse

What the chapter actually says
Chapter summary
  1. Start your purse to fattening: save at least a tenth of what you earn
  2. Control your expenses: separate needs from wants
  3. Make your gold multiply: invest your savings
  4. Guard your treasures from loss: only invest where it is safe
  5. Make your home a profitable investment
  6. Insuring a future income
  7. Increase your ability to earn
My reflection
In my own words

The cure that stood out to me was insuring your future income. As someone that does not have alot of money at times it is good to make sure that I am budgetting to try to not go past my own needs.

5 Laws of Gold

What the chapter says
Chapter summary
  1. Save at least a tenth of your earnings
  2. Put your money to work through wise investment
  3. Invest under the advice of people who know
  4. Do not invest in things you do not understand
  5. Avoid get-rich-quick schemes and unrealistic returns
My reflection
In my own words

The book teaches that having money means nothing if you don't know the habits and discipline to keep it, because without understanding how to save, invest wisely, and avoid stupid decisions, you'll lose it all no matter how much you start with.

Law 5 reminds me to stay away from "too good to be true" offers, which is basically what phishing and online investment scams are built on. Law 3 pushes me to ask people with experience before making big decisions, the same way a more experienced person in my field Cybersecurity would escalate an issue when they are unsure how to handle an issue.

My Biggest Takeaway

I need to try and build the foundation of my savings by just starting.

This sets the foundation for me so that I know not to go overboard on the spending
